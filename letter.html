For My Meena

A Little Letter

Dear Meena,

I don't actually know how to start this letter.

Every year when your birthday comes, I think I'll write something different and something really nice. But when I actually sit down to write, I realise I have so many things to say that I don't know where to start.

If someone had told me in 2022, when I first saw you in college, that you would become this important to me, I definitely wouldn't have believed them.

At first, you were just someone I met in college.

Then somehow, without even realising it, you became the person I wanted to tell everything to.

From all our random conversations to the stupid things we laugh about, from our small fights to the way we become normal again after them... somehow all those little things became some of my favourite memories.

And you know one thing?

When I'm with you, I don't really think about taking photos.

My phone will literally be in my hand, but I'll forget about it.

Because I'm too busy talking to you, walking with you, laughing with you, or just being there with you.

That's why maybe we don't have thousands of perfect pictures together.

But I have so many moments in my head that I don't need a photo to remember them.

I remember walking with you.
I remember holding your hand.
I remember our silly fights.
I remember the times we just sat together without even having anything important to talk about.

And somehow, even those silent moments felt nice.

There are some people with whom you have to constantly do something to enjoy their company.

But with you, sometimes just being there is enough.

That's something I don't know how to explain properly.

After college, when everything started changing and we didn't know where life was going to take us, I honestly didn't know how things would be between us.

But somehow life brought us to Bangalore too.

Same city.
Different work.
Different routines.
But still finding our own little time together.

And whenever I get to spend time with you, everything feels a little lighter.

I don't know what our future is going to look like.

I don't know where we'll be, what jobs we'll have, which city we'll live in, or how much our lives will change.

But I really hope that even after all those changes, there will still be a you and me somewhere in it.

Maybe we'll fight.
Maybe we'll irritate each other.
Maybe we'll have days when we don't understand each other.

But I hope we never become strangers.

Because you have become such a special part of my life that I can't imagine my college memories without you.

And I don't want my future memories to be without you either.

♡ ✦ ♡

So this birthday, I don't want to give you just something that you can keep in a cupboard.

I wanted to give you a few things that actually came from me.

A little webpage.
A song.
Some gifts.
And this letter.

But honestly, none of these things can properly explain what you mean to me.

So I'll just say this in the simplest way I know.

Thank you for being there.

Thank you for all the laughs.

Thank you for all the memories.

Thank you for understanding me even when I don't explain myself properly.

Thank you for being someone with whom I can be completely myself.

And thank you for becoming one of the most beautiful parts of my life without me even realising when it happened.

One day we'll look back at these Bangalore days and probably laugh about how we were at this age, where we worked, where we stayed, what we fought about, and all the stupid things we did.

I just hope when we look back, we'll still be together in those memories.

Your birthday is October 7.
Mine is October 8.

Two birthdays next to each other.

So maybe that's another little coincidence that belongs to us. ❤️

Happy Birthday, Meena.

I hope this year gives you everything you're working for.

I hope you laugh a lot.
I hope you stay happy.
And I hope I get to annoy you for many, many more years. 😂

Whatever happens in life, I just want one thing to remain the same—

our bond.

Don't change that.

And if life gives us many more birthdays to celebrate together,

I'll be there for them too.

Happy Birthday, my Meena. ❤️

With lots of love,

Sathya
